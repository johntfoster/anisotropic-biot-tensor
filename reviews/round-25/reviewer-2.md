## 4. Physics assessment

- **Pore-fabric anisotropy construction.** \(\mathbf F=\mathbf R_A\mathbf G^{1/2}\bar{\mathbf F}\), \(\mathbf G=a^{2/3}\mathbf H\) with \(\det\mathbf H=1\), is a standard volumetric–isochoric split of the multiplicative distention, and \(\mathbf H=(\det\mathbf G)^{-1/3}\mathbf G\) correctly plays the Cowin fabric-tensor role (eigenvalues are squared principal shape ratios). The conformal specialization \(\mathbf G=a^{2/3}\mathbf I\) reduces exactly to the earlier spherical distention, including cancellation of \(\mathbf R_A\). Sound.
- **Transversely isotropic restriction.** With \(\mathbf H=h^{-2}\mathbf m\otimes\mathbf m+h(\mathbf I-\mathbf m\otimes\mathbf m)\), \(\det\mathbf H=1\) with eigenvalues \(h^{-2},h,h\); substituting \(\mathbf E_{\mathrm{dis}}=(\ln a/3)\mathbf I+\ln h(\tfrac12\mathbf I-\tfrac32\mathbf m\otimes\mathbf m)\) into \(\exp\) returns \(a^{1/3}h^{-1}\), \(a^{1/3}h^{1/2}\), i.e. \(\mathbf G=a^{2/3}\mathbf H\). The claim that the retained volumetric–axial subspace is exact (not a projection) is correct, and the manuscript's corrected description of the shape scalar as \(\ln h\) (the log of the transverse eigenvalue) is the right one. This is precisely what Finding 1 shows the evidence file still gets wrong.
- **Role of the volume–axial distention modulus.** The recorded limits confirm the physics: `fabric-verification.json` `limits` gives Biot anisotropy \(1.1\times10^{-16}\) (no coupling), \(-1.1\times10^{-16}\) (frozen shape), and \(-0.0596\) with coupling. So an isotropic mineral plus a fabric with zero volume–axial coupling keeps \(\mathbf B\) spherical, and the directional coupling \(B_\parallel-B_\perp\) is carried by that modulus. The manuscript's statements in `sec:fabric-transverse`, the `fig:fe-fabric-probe` caption, and the limitations entry are consistent with this evidence.
- **Drained/undrained limits and Mandel.** The drained compliance restriction is applied (not assumed) in the reference decks, the analytical reference passes 38 checks including exact drained/undrained limits and drainage-boundary identities, and the finite-deformation comparison is correctly labeled a demonstration with a stated discretization floor rather than a verified limit. The finite-load and manufacturing claims are stated at the strength the artifacts support (no order above one asserted; no mesh-convergence claim for the fabric runs).
