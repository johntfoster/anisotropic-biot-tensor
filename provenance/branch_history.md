## Author-directed development since the parent revision

The work that produced this independent paper was conducted in an
author-directed chat session after the parent revision. The session first
reviewed the parent manuscript's energy-consistent skeleton and mineral
response derivation, then considered whether solid mass balance forces a
scalar Biot coefficient. The resulting distinction was that mass balance
relates \(\bar J\), \(J\), and solid volume fraction but does not by itself
restrict the constitutive response to \(\bar J(J,p)\).

The session then developed a self-contained one-solid/one-fluid theoretical
extension. It retained the distention--true-deformation kinematics needed for
\(\bar J\), derived the finite-deformation Biot tensor from pressure
conjugacy and a Legendre transform, and introduced a small-strain
generalized-Hooke specialization with independent drained-skeleton, mineral,
and skeleton--mineral coupling daughter tensors. The paper records the
anisotropic special cases and the calibrated fully isotropic recovery
\(B=1-K/K_s\).

Three independent peer reviewers audited the draft through multiple revision
cycles. Their reviews corrected the finite-deformation conjugacy to use
\(\bar J\), made the mineral-state coordinate split explicit, separated the
volumetric calibration that recovers the classical coefficient from a broad
quadratic family, and
verified the reference-density normalization that maps Foster and Xu's scalar
coefficient to the mixture-normalized form used here. The final review cycle
returned three accept recommendations.

This document is a sanitized, durable summary of that development history;
it is not a verbatim transcript of the private chat.
