# Fixed-density effective stress

Verified on 2026-09-22 for the definition preceding
`eq:total-cauchy-single-prime` in `main.tex`.

The local full text, `references/pdfs/fosterxu2025.pdf`, treats solid
internal energy as a function of skeleton deformation and solid intrinsic
density in equation (19), defines the single-prime nominal stress in
equation (21), and explicitly identifies its energy derivative at fixed
intrinsic specific volume on page 11. Fixed intrinsic specific volume is
equivalent to fixed solid intrinsic density. The mixture Cauchy stress
relation on page 10 supplies the corresponding spatial stress form.
These passages support the revised definition.

Publisher metadata confirms Foster and Xu, Journal of the Mechanics and
Physics of Solids 204 (2025), 106263, DOI
10.1016/j.jmps.2025.106263. The local author PDF bears a June 22, 2026 date;
the equation and page evidence above refers to that local version.
